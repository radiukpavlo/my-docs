# YouTube upload notes

Recommended category: Science & Technology. Education is also appropriate for a teaching-focused channel. Set the video language to English and audience to not made for kids, matching this specialist academic talk. Use a research or medical imaging AI playlist.

Upload as unlisted first, review the completed HD processing, audio, and on-screen slide readability, then publish. Review English captions against the actual recording, especially author names, ACDC, M&Ms, WEDD, decimal values, and units. The speaker script is a reference, not a time-aligned transcript.

Add links to the manuscript and slides only when stable public URLs exist. Do not use local filesystem paths or substitute the Bernard reference as this study's DOI. Do not claim the recording was delivered at a conference unless that is accurate.

Use actual recording timestamps for chapters. Suggested chapter labels: Introduction; The audit gap; Cohorts and objectives; Framework and evaluated release; Semantic reconstruction; Rules and abstention; External domain shift; Coverage and accuracy; Predicate alignment and robustness; Conclusions. YouTube requires a first timestamp of 00:00, at least three chapters in ascending order, and a minimum chapter duration of 10 seconds. No timestamps have been invented for this package.

YouTube's current guidance recommends 16:9 JPG/PNG thumbnails at 3840 x 2160, with a minimum width of 640 pixels. Desktop upload limit: 50 MB; mobile video-thumbnail limit: 2 MB. A verified account is needed for uploading custom thumbnails.

Tags are secondary: prioritize the title, thumbnail, opening description, and accurate captions. Keep tags in the tags field, rather than adding a keyword dump to the description.

For a recording of your real speech and slides, discussing AI does not itself require an altered-content label. Complete the disclosure accurately if realistic synthetic or meaningfully altered people, voices, or events appear. AI assistance with scripts or thumbnails alone is listed by YouTube among production assistance that does not require disclosure.
