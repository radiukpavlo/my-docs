           Khmelnytskyi–Dresden Exchange Visit

                                14–16 September 2026

                      Delegation of the City of Khmelnytskyi,
                  Khmelnytskyi National University (KhNU) and
Leonid Yuzkov Khmelnytskyi University of Management and Law (LYKUML)

                   Strengthening cooperation through
  the Dresden–Stuttgart–Khmelnytskyi Solidarity Partnership and
the EU-funded project U_CAN | Ukraine towards Carbon Neutrality

Program Overview

14 September 2026 Welcome and Partnership Exchange – Reception at the City of Dresden,
                             introduction to the Dresden–Khmelnytskyi cooperation, U_CAN project
                             exchange and networking activities

15 September 2026  Sustainable Mobility and Adaptive Traffic Management – Exchange on
                   smart mobility solutions, traffic management and the U_CAN pilot project

16 September 2026  Waste Management and Circular Economy – Technical exchange on waste
                   management, resource recovery and climate-neutral urban development

German–Ukrainian interpretation is provided for selected programme sessions.
