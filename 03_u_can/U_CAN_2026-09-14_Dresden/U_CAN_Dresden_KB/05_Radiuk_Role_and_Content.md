## Commitment 1 — Day 01, Mon 14 Sep, 15:00–16:30
### U_CAN Scenario Stress-Testing Workshop (TU Dresden, BZW Wing B)

Run by **Swati Kulashri**. She wrote a detailed briefing on 09.09.2026 (thread `1a0827a57cbba97d`):

**Background.** A Scenario Building workshop was already held with **Pavlo** and the Deputy Mayor of
Khmelnytskyi. It produced **four plausible futures** for the adaptive traffic systems pilot, exploring how
combinations of *AI-infrastructure affordability* and *long-term funding security* would influence
implementation, stakeholder roles and upscaling.

**This session.** A **technology shock** is introduced:

> *Can adaptive traffic systems continue if AI/digital technologies become unavailable, unaffordable,
> or technically unreliable?*

The workshop also explores how three actors jointly keep the pilot alive under that shock:
- **KhNU** — technology expertise
- **Khmelnytskyi City Council** — political/policy perspective, experience with green transport systems
- **LYKUML** — its dissemination centre

**The three framing questions per scenario:**
1. **What breaks?** — which part of the pilot or strategy becomes vulnerable?
2. **What still works?** — which actions, capacities or partnerships remain useful despite the shock?
3. **What should we change now?** — which adaptation or institutional measure would make the pilot more resilient?

**Goal:** validated, resilient pathways for pilot implementation and replication.
Swati will re-summarise the four scenarios at the start of the session.

**Preparation for Pavlo:** be ready to speak to the technical side of each of the four scenarios —
degradation modes of the adaptive traffic system, non-AI fallbacks, what parts of the stack are
affordable/replaceable, and which institutional measures at KhNU would harden the pilot.
