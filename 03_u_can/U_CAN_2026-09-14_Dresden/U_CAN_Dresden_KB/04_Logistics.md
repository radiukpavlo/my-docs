## 3. Getting around Dresden

The programme uses **public transport (DVB/VVO trams, buses, local trains)**; most city-centre
locations are within walking distance and the group walks whenever convenient.

### Fares (Dresden fare zone, as quoted 09.09.2026)

| Ticket | Price | Validity |
|---|---|---|
| Single journey | **€3.60** | up to 1 hour within Dresden |
| 4-trip ticket | **€13.00** | four individual journeys — *recommended option* |
| One-day pass | **€9.50** | unlimited until 04:00 the next day |

- Ticket machines: around **Dresden Hauptbahnhof** and at **Prager Straße**, directly in front of the Stadtforum.
- **Paper tickets must be validated/stamped in the bus/tram when the journey starts.**
- Info (EN): https://www.dvb.de/en-gb/tickets-en/ticket-finder and https://www.dvb.de/en-gb/tickets/ticket-overview/
- **Have a valid ticket before the first joint tram ride on Monday.**
