## What the project actually does (GA abstract, condensed)

U_CAN runs a practical **bottom-up** process bringing Ukrainian cities towards
climate neutrality, following European standards and the European Green Deal.
It starts from a core group of **8 Ukrainian cities**, **Dresden as a Mission City**,
NGOs and research institutions. Over 48 months the CNSC-UA twinning partners
exchange experience with further Ukrainian municipalities, adapting to European
climate standards with experts such as DIN.

The mechanism is: vision workshops → road mapping → stakeholder co-creation →
twinning of European and Ukrainian cities → **use-case development in each pilot
city** → **pilot implementation** of mitigation and adaptation measures, wrapped in
an extensive dissemination, exploitation and communication plan. In light of the
war, co-creation and **Citizen Science**-augmented training keep the approach
effective, value-based and needs-oriented.

**Keywords (official):** architecture, smart buildings, smart cities, urban
engineering; climate change mitigation; spatial development and architecture, land
use, regional planning; facilitation tools, network development, network design,
stakeholder co-creation, road mapping, pilot implementation, standardisation.

> **Careful:** the abstract's "8 Ukrainian cities" is the *core city group*, which is
> wider than the **6 WP4 pilot cities**. See [09_Open_Items.md](09_Open_Items.md).
