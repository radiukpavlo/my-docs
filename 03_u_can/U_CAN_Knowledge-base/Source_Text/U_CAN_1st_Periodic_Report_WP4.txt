WP4 Objectives

 The aim of WP4 is to implement short to medium-term climate mitigation strategies in
 6 pilot cities in Ukraine (Lviv, Kyiv, Zhytomyr, Khmelnytskyi, Ivano Frankivsk and
 Vinnytsia). The specific objectives are:
 · Support implementation of pilot activities in six cities to demonstrate climate-

    neutral city solutions; and
 · Integrate learnings from project and contemporary practices in short and medium-

    term strategies of local governing bodies in Ukraine.
