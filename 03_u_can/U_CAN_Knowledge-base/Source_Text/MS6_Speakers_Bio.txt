Biography
Swati Kulashri is a research associate at the WISSENSARCHITEKTUR – Laboratory of Knowledge Architecture at Technische Universität Dresden and the coordinator of U_CAN, the Horizon Europe project supporting Ukrainian cities on their path towards climate neutrality. Her current work sits at the intersection of urban development, participation, knowledge transfer and collaborative city-making. Public TU Dresden project records also place her within Smart City Dresden and Urban Co-Design activities, where she has contributed to participatory methods and digital tools for urban strategy and co-design.
Kulashri’s conference contribution is especially relevant because U_CAN is designed to connect Ukrainian municipalities with European research, policy and city-level practice. Her role requires coordinating a complex international partnership while translating climate goals into practical frameworks that cities can use in conditions of recovery and reconstruction. That combination of strategic oversight and hands-on project delivery makes her a strong voice on how climate-neutral urban development can be advanced through cooperation, capacity building and institutional learning. At this conference she brings a European project perspective grounded in practical support for cities facing urgent mobility, resilience and decarbonisation challenges.
