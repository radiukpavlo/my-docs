## WP4 objectives and tasks

**Aim:** implement short- to medium-term climate mitigation strategies in the
6 pilot cities. Specific objectives: (a) support pilot activities in six cities to
demonstrate climate-neutral city solutions; (b) integrate learnings from the project
and contemporary practice into the short- and medium-term strategies of Ukrainian
local governing bodies.

## Where the other pilots stand (06.07.2026) — useful for peer context

- **Lviv (CIL):** two-stage PED concept (micro-PEDs → virtual PEDs); One-Stop-Shop
  analysis based on Swedish practice; Lviv City Office in Brussels being formed.
- **Kyiv (ISKPI):** modified morphological analysis of 8 planned road tunnels;
  Tunnel 5 selected; modelled vehicle-emission reduction **4.4–5.4 %**, up to
  **592 t/day**; metro assessment framework in progress.
- **Zhytomyr (ZCC):** council decision 16.10.2025 permitting land documentation for a
  floating solar plant; memorandum with Ocean Sun AS and Hicc LLC (Decision No. 1497);
  agreements with Leo Energy LLC and KATP-0628; land-use documentation received June 2026.
- **Ivano-Frankivsk (BRIT):** public PV monitoring platform live at
  <http://asempv.escoua.com/en/p/ucan>; climate indicators + 3-year baseline agreed
  with the city; peer learning with Wrocław and Košice.
- **Vinnytsia (ME IUD):** sociological and hydrological/biodiversity studies of the
  Tyazhylivka river; spatial concept and sketches; public presentation January 2026.
