## Folders

- `Reference/` — read-only PDFs (decks, reports, agendas). PDF renditions are kept
  instead of the PPTX originals: same content, ~8× smaller.
- `Editable/` — the few files you actually edit: slide template, speech script,
  progress tracker, scenario/Q&A documents.
- `Source_Text/` — plain-text extracts of every heavy source (Grant Agreement,
  Consortium Agreement, reports, decks). **Grep these first** — they are cheap to
  read and cover all the facts in the binaries.
- `Playbooks/` — the proven prompts used to produce U_CAN slides, speech scripts,
  conference reports, timesheets and travel paperwork.

## Ground rules for anything produced from this KB

1. **British English**, **Avenir** font family, U_CAN blue/green/yellow palette.
2. CO₂ figures from KhNU work are **simulation-based potential**, never claimed as
   achieved real-world reductions in Khmelnytskyi. See [04](04_KhNU_Pilot.md).
3. Every public output carries the EU funding statement and disclaimer from
   [07_Boilerplate.md](07_Boilerplate.md).
4. D4.5 is **not yet submitted** — say "evidence base advanced", never "delivered".
