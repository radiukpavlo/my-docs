### Scientific Results

Include a concise research-results section. Mention two key publications:

1. **“An Adaptive Machine Learning Approach to Sustainable Traffic Planning: High-Fidelity Pattern Recognition in Smart Transportation Systems”**, Future Transportation, 2025, Gold Open Access, Q2. It used **HDBSCAN** and **k-means** clustering with a data-driven weighted voting mechanism for high-fidelity traffic-pattern recognition. It was validated on a Khmelnytskyi transport-network simulation, with scenario identification accuracy of approximately **92.8–95.0%**, V-measure around **0.79–0.82**, and compactness improvement of **10–14%** over standalone algorithms.

2. **“Topography-Aware Deep Reinforcement Learning with Contextual Reward Engineering for Sustainable and Efficient Urban Traffic Control”**, Future Transportation, 2026, Gold Open Access, Q2. It presented a topography-aware deep reinforcement-learning framework with **Context-Specific Reward Design (CSRD)**. The simulation used a **3.8-degree uphill approach**, **14,800 vehicles over 9 hours**, and a **20% heavy-vehicle fleet** with HBEFA emission profiles in a **SUMO + DRL** environment. In simulation, the specialised topography-aware controller reduced CO₂ emissions to **256.97 million mg**, achieving **8.6% reduction vs. MaxPressure** and **4.7% reduction vs. standard DQN**, with **72.09 s** mean trip duration and **1.31 vehicles** average queue length. State these as simulation results, not field deployment outcomes.
