## What happens in the room

1. Swati re-summarises the **four futures** built at the L'Aquila Scenario Building workshop (July 2026).
2. She introduces a **technology shock**:
   > *Can adaptive traffic systems continue if AI/digital technologies become unavailable, unaffordable, or technically unreliable?*
3. For **each of the four scenarios**, the room brainstorms three questions:
   1. **What breaks?** — which part of the pilot or strategy becomes vulnerable
   2. **What still works?** — which actions, capacities or partnerships remain useful despite the shock
   3. **What should we change now?** — which adaptation or institutional measure makes the pilot more resilient
4. Goal: **validated, resilient pathways for pilot implementation and replication.**

Expect A0 printed sheets, sticky notes and markers (that was the L'Aquila format), and possibly a Miro board.

## Pavlo's 60-second opening (if asked to set the scene)

> "Our pilot is deliberately built in four layers: platform, data, algorithms, scale. Only the third layer is AI. The technology shock removes that layer — it does not remove the other three. So our answer to the shock is a **degradation ladder**: full adaptive AI, then frozen-model adaptive, then actuated control, then time-of-day fixed-time plans, then manual. Our SUMO digital twin lets us regenerate good fixed-time plans **without AI and without live data**, and our published research puts a number on what we lose: the AI layer is worth about **8.6 % CO₂** over the best non-AI baseline. That is a real benefit, but it is not the whole pilot. What we must protect now is the open-API procurement clause, local data storage, and the KhNU–City partnership — because those are what make the AI layer re-attachable later."

---

## Three things to insist on before the session ends

1. **The open-API / AI-compatibility procurement clause** — zero cost today, preserves every future. Robust in all four scenarios.
2. **The digital twin as a permanent, AI-independent asset** — it keeps producing value even at zero budget.
3. **The degradation ladder written into the technical specification** — so "AI fails" never means "signals fail".
