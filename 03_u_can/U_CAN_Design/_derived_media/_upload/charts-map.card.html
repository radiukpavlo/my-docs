The pilot-cities map

Six pilot cities on a map of Ukraine is U_CAN's signature graphic. The WP7 artwork has now been located and is in assets/maps/ — but only as raster. This card carries both: what you can use today, and the contract a vector rebuild must meet.
Found. WP7…/Communication materials/Maps/ holds the real artwork in five colourways, 1024 × 537 PNG. Ukraine in grey, the six pilot oblasts filled, yellow dots on the pilot cities, other cities as navy dots. Only one variant carries city labels; the other four are unlabelled so a layout can set its own type.
Also supplied: light-blue-no-names, light-green-no-names, dark-blue-no-names. The six pilot cities are Lviv, Ivano-Frankivsk, Khmelnytskyi, Vinnytsia, Zhytomyr and Kyiv — always list them in text near the figure, never leave the map as the only place a fact appears.
No vector master exists. Both project Drive folders were searched in full — 3 319 files. The only SVGs anywhere are the eleven LHD partner logos. The 19 MB rolldown banners are single flat rasters, the pilot postcards are rasters, and D2.1_U_CAN_Interactive map is a report about the map whose figures are screenshots. At 1024 px the map is ~124 dpi at A4 — fine projected at 1280 px, too coarse to print.
Two ways to close this. (1) Ask WP7 comms for the Illustrator source — five clean colourways of one artwork is the signature of a single .ai with swapped swatches, so it exists; it has simply never been shared into the archive. That also gives UkraineMap the oblast paths it needs. (2) Rebuild from open oblast geodata, which needs a decision on the source and its licence. Until then use the PNGs on screen and keep the map out of print deliverables at A4.

Specification — what a vector rebuild must meet

ElementRule
Base landmass--chart-seq-1 #E3EDFA fill, --chart-grid 1px stroke. The country is a ground, not a data layer — it must not compete with the markers.
Oblast boundariesShown only when oblast-level data is being encoded. Otherwise omitted — they add 24 lines and no information. Note the supplied artwork fills whole oblasts rather than marking points; a rebuild should keep that, since the pilots are oblast-scale programmes.
Pilot city markerFilled circle, --ucan-blue, 10px radius, 2px white halo so it survives on any base tint.
Mission (twinned) cityHollow circle, --ucan-blue 2px stroke, white fill. Shape distinguishes the two categories, not colour.
LabelsAlways visible — never hover-only. --chart-value-label, 12px, 600 weight, offset 12px right of the marker, with a 3px white text-halo.
Bilingual namesEnglish name first, Ukrainian in the same label tagged lang="uk", e.g. "Khmelnytskyi / Хмельницький".
Intensity / choropleth--chart-seq-1 → --chart-seq-6, six steps maximum. The two lightest steps need a --chart-grid hairline or the shape loses its edge against the page.
FormatSVG, always. A map in a deliverable gets printed at A4 and projected at 1280px from the same file. The supplied PNG does not meet this and is an interim.
Accessibilityrole="img" plus a list of the six cities in text near the figure.

Marker key

Why shape and not colour. The two categories sit on the same map at the same size; if they differed only in hue, a greyscale print of the deliverable would merge them. Filled versus hollow survives greyscale, photocopying and colour blindness alike.
